BIOGRAPHY

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. My research interests include multimodal AI, computer vision, video understanding, natural language processing, generative AI, and trustworthy AI.

My undergraduate thesis focused on explainable multimodal AI for misleading short-video detection , integrating visual, speech, and textual information through cross-modal learning. This work strengthened my interest in multimodal content understanding, AI-generated content analysis, and explainable AI.

I gained practical experience as a Computer Vision Analysis Intern at Brain Machine AI , where I worked on AI systems involving computer vision, multimodal learning, and large language models.

As an Instructor for Shared Task Hunt 2026 at the Andromeda Space & Robotics Research Organization (ASRRO), I delivered research training in machine learning, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, and LLMs, and mentored research teams whose work resulted in multiple papers accepted at international AI workshops and conferences. I have also contributed to the research community through academic peer review for international NLP and multimodal AI venues.

I also served as Programming Secretary (Machine Learning) of the CUET Computer Club , contributing to machine-learning workshops, technical events, research-oriented training, coding competitions, student mentoring, and engagement in artificial intelligence research on campus.

Moving forward, I aim to pursue graduate research and develop reliable multimodal AI systems that can reason across visual, linguistic, and audio information. My long-term research interests center on building intelligent systems that are capable, generalizable, interpretable, safe, and trustworthy .
